Introduction

What POLYMIRA is and what it does.

POLYMIRA is a full-screen frontend for Windows that turns a PC into something that behaves like a game console. It collects the games you already have — store games and emulated console games — into one menu, starts them, and gives you a console-style menu on top of them while they run.

What it does

  • Finds games from Steam, Epic Games, the Xbox app, GOG, EA, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net and Riot, plus ROMs and disc images for emulated systems.
  • Downloads emulators from their official sources and configures their controller settings before a game starts.
  • Opens an in-game menu on the controller's Guide button: pause, screenshot, quick save, performance, quit.
  • Keeps play time, trophies and save backups for everything in the library.

What it does not do

  • It does not include or download games, BIOS files, firmware or encryption keys.
  • It does not replace Steam or other launchers. Store games still start through their own launchers.
  • It does not move, copy or modify your game files.
