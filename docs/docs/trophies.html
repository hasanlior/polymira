Trophies and statistics

Achievements, play time and notifications.

Steam achievements

Read from the files the Steam client keeps on disk. No account or key is needed. A game's detail page shows its progress; the full list is under Home → Trophies.

RetroAchievements

For emulated games, add your RetroAchievements username and Web API key in Settings → Game Mode & System → RetroAchievements account. The key is on your RetroAchievements settings page.

Notifications

Unlocked trophies, finished downloads, backups and updates appear as cards in the top right corner, also over a running game. Turn the in-game cards off with Notifications while playing. Past notifications are under Home → Notifications.

Play statistics

Home → Play statistics shows the last seven days, weekly and total play time, and your most played games.
